# Caption: CAGED carousel (English)

CAGED for playing by ear: find any melody on the neck.

1. Hear home: the note the tune ends on
2. Drop the nearest CAGED shape on it
3. Try the root, 3rd and 5th first: melodies lean on chord tones
4. Missed? The next note is 1–2 frets away, inside the pentatonic box
5. Chord changed? Its shape is right there

Try it on Ode to Joy (slide 8), then on your next song. Save this and send it to a friend who still plays from tabs.
