# Caption: pentatonic carousel (draft, English)

Play any melody by ear with one shape. A method for beginners.

1. Find home: the note the tune keeps coming back to
2. Place the pentatonic box on it
3. Find the notes inside the box

Worked example: Amazing Grace (public domain), key of G. Save this for your next jam.

Early access for guitarists → link in profile.
