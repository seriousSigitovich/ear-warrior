# 3 Frusciante-style solos (20 s)

Regenerate: `../../guitar-rig/.venv/bin/python frusciante.py [id]`. `.mid` = rhythm guitar (ch0) + lead (ch2), no drums.
Rhythm guitar: `crunch` preset, doubled L/R. Lead: new `lead` preset (less gain and low end than `metal`), dotted-eighth delay.
Lead range goes up to D6 (a Strat's 22nd fret), bends stay within ±2 semitones so the MIDI export is exact.

| # | Key / chords | BPM | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 cathedral | D minor: Dm Bb F C / Dm Bb A7 Dm | 100 | 4 bars of long bent notes and wide leaps, then falling pentatonic threes, rising fours over Bb, a Phrygian-dominant fall over A7, held C6→D6 bend |
| 02 firestorm | E minor: Em C G D ×2 / Am B7 / Em | 128 | call and answer, sextuplet bursts, an Em-pentatonic ladder, descending minor thirds over G, D#5 over B7, held B5 bend |
| 03 sunburst | B minor: Bm G D A / G A Bm | 88 | one motif climbing a third per bar, A-major arpeggio, four sextuplet sweeps over G, chromatic fall over A, held D6 |

Tone and balance were set by numbers (LUFS, peaks), not by ear.
