# What the top "how to grow on YouTube Shorts" videos say (2026-10-17)

How this was made: YouTube Data API `search.list` (20 queries about Shorts growth, `order=viewCount`,
4–20 min videos, last 12–18 months) → 595 unique videos → 50 picked by hand (dropped comedy Shorts,
Hindi clones, repeats from one channel, "AI automation" get-rich videos) and ranked by views.
Transcripts via `scripts/research/youtube-transcript.mjs`: **31 of 50 had English captions**; the other 19
are Hindi-original or have no speech track. **25 were read in full**, 6 more were fetched but not read
(monetization / long-form angle). Quota used ≈ 2 000 of 10 000 daily units.

**Caveat on the sample:** most authors sell a course or an AI-clipping tool (affiliate codes, Gumroad,
"academy"), the case studies are their own and unverified, and none of them is in a music/education niche.
Weight claims that come with a mechanism (Kallaway, vidIQ, the Tartarotti interview) over bare tips.

## What most of them agree on

1. **Testing in waves.** A new Short is shown to a small sample (Kallaway: ~200 mostly non-followers; others say 1 K, then 10 K / 30 K). Good
   data → next wave, mixed → resample, bad → stop. The "view jails" at ~0, ~1 K, ~10 K, ~30 K are just the wave you failed. Hals, Kallaway, Dan,
   Creatie, Ryvahn, Hustle Guy, John Scott, vidIQ.
2. **One narrow topic for one audience.** Mixed topics blur who the sample should be, so later videos get a worse sample. Kallaway, Danny Why,
   Dan, Passive Hustler, Creatie, Ryvahn (6 sources, the most repeated claim).
3. **The first 1–4 seconds decide it.** Say what the video is about in the first 1–2 s (no warm-up), address "you", use a contrast
   (what you believe vs. what I show). A visual hook + a spoken hook + a text hook together. Kallaway, Luc Boulch ("4-second drop"),
   John Scott, E'Calm, Creatie, Deven Seenath.
4. **The metric that matters is "viewed vs swiped away" and retention.** Quoted winners: 72–96 % viewed, average view duration at or above the video
   length (loops). Tartarotti's interview: 75 % → 80 % average viewed can be the gap between 100 K and 10 M views. Deven, Isaac, Ryvahn, Hals.
5. **Short beats long.** Cut every line that does not lead to the reaction; a 15 s tutorial beats a 40 s one. John Scott, Deven, Tartarotti
   (loop-friendly videos).
6. **Pick the reaction first** (LOL, WTF, wow, aw, "so true"), then write. People share reactions, not information. John Scott.
7. **Comments are a signal.** Same views, 9 vs 132 comments → very different push (Hals). Take a side, or make people open the comments
   (Kallaway, Tartarotti).
8. **Format jail at ~30 K:** the idea is fine, the presentation looks like everyone else's. Fix: import a format that already works in an adjacent
   niche (Minecraft format → Roblox), add your twist. John Scott ("outliers"), Hustle Guy ("niche bending"), Ryvahn.
9. **End right after the payoff.** No "thanks for watching" / subscribe plea. Luc Boulch, E'Calm, vidIQ.

## Where they contradict each other (treat as noise)

- Upload time, hashtags, descriptions: YT Biceps (3.5 M views) says they matter; Kallaway (1.1 M) says they do not. Kallaway has the mechanism.
- Warm up a new account for 5–7 days (Creatie, Ryvahn, E'Calm) vs. "doesn't matter" (Luc Boulch).
- Posting cadence: daily (Ryvahn, E'Calm), every 48 h (Passive Hustler), "daily Shorts kill channels" (titles only, no transcript). The 100-Shorts-in-a-day
  test (triste): 62 of 100 videos under 100 views, upload cap hit at 61.
- Hook with context vs. "anti-hook" that drops straight into the scene (Deven, citing Zach D. Films). Both agree: no delay.
