Questions

Do I need to know music theory?

No. You need a few chords and to be able to play something from a tab. Every bit of theory in the course comes with a shape on the neck and a tune to try it on.

I think I just don’t have an ear.

Almost nobody starts with one. Playing by ear uses relative pitch — hearing the distance between notes — and that is trained, not born. Module 0 gets you your first melody in ten minutes.
