Delete your account

For the Ear Warrior app. Signing in is optional, so you only have an account if you chose to sign in with your email.

In the app (immediately)

Open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. The deletion takes effect at once and the app returns to anonymous use.

Without the app (within 7 days)

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address you signed in with and ask for the account to be deleted:

golovsha@gmail.com

We complete the request within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and your account record.
  • Every sign-in session, and the link between your account and the phone(s) you used. After this, usage events and crash reports from those phones are no longer connected to you.
  • Any sign-in codes issued to your email address.

What stays

  • Anonymous practice data tied only to a random device identifier, which cannot be traced back to you once the link is removed.
  • Practice history stored on your phone — uninstalling the app removes it.

If you also joined the waitlist on this website, ask us to remove that entry in the same email. More detail is in the privacy policy.
