Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026. This policy covers the website earwarrior.app and its waitlist, and the optional email sign-in of the Ear Warrior app.

What we collect

  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist: your email address, the time you signed up, and a short "source" tag taken from the link you came from (for example youtube:ear16, from the utm_source / ref parameters), so we know which video brought you. Our notification email and database entry may also include your browser's user-agent string.
  • Visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics. It counts page views and records the referring page, country, device type, browser and operating system. It sets no cookies and does not track you across other sites or build a profile of you.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider processes your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

We do not collect audio, payment details, or anything else through this website.

The app: optional email sign-in

The Ear Warrior app works fully without an account. If you choose to sign in, we collect:

  • Your email address, which we verify by sending you a one-time 6-digit code. There is no password.
  • An account identifier generated by us, and the time the account was created.
  • A link between your account and the random device identifier the app creates on your phone. While you are signed in, this lets us attach your email to that phone’s anonymous usage events (including events recorded on the same phone before you signed in) and to its crash reports, so we can tell testers apart and follow up with you about feedback. Signing out ends the link for everything recorded afterwards.
  • A sign-in code is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form and expires after 10 minutes; your session token is stored on your phone in its secure storage and, on our server, only in hashed form.

Processors for this: our own server and database; the mail service that delivers the code to you; and our crash-reporting service, which receives your email together with crash reports while you are signed in. If you do not sign in, the app sends only anonymous practice data tied to a random device identifier, as before.

Deleting your account. In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Without the app: see how to delete your account; we complete such requests within 7 days. Deletion removes your email, your account record, your sessions and the link to your phone(s).

Why

To let you know when Ear Warrior launches, to count how many people are interested, to learn which of our videos and posts are worth making more of, and to keep the site running. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Who processes it

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting and Web Analytics for this site.
  • Upstash — the database that stores the waitlist (email, signup order, source tag).
  • Our email provider — delivers the signup notification to us.
  • Our own server and database — a copy of the waitlist.

These providers may process data outside your country (including in the United States).

How long we keep it

Waitlist data is kept until we launch and have told you about it, or until you ask us to delete it. Visit statistics are kept in aggregate by Vercel according to its own retention rules.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the data we hold about you (including an app account), or to remove you from the waitlist at any time, by emailing golovsha@gmail.com. Tell us the email address you signed up with.

Our own social media reporting

To measure our videos, we use the official YouTube and Instagram APIs to read read-only analytics of our own Ear Warrior accounts (views, likes, comments, follower counts and similar statistics). This is done by us, for internal reporting only. The data is stored in our private database and is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. It is not about visitors to this site, and we do not request access to anyone else's Google or Instagram account. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
